## Limitations

- Nutrition macros are manual optional values; without all values, totals are partial. No calorie target is provided.
- A timer may be paused/resumed and restarted, but it cannot be kept active for over 7 days without saving; discard/reset if time limits are exceeded.
- No OS background timer service, push notifications or cloud sync. The persisted timer is reconciled when the PWA is opened; if the device clock changes, the app may reject invalid backward timestamps.
- Timer sessions are read-only in measured duration, but category/note may be changed; manual sessions have editable durations.
- This ZIP is an implementation package, **not a production release**.
